How to Connect Your Square Account (5-Minute Guide)

Follow these quick steps to get your Square credentials and start accepting payments.

1

Log in to Square Developer Dashboard

Open Square Developer Portal (developer.squareup.com) and sign in with your regular Square merchant login.

2

Create an Application

Click "+ New Application" (or select an existing one). Name it e.g. Taozijia Photography Website.

3

Copy Application ID and Access Token

Under Credentials, toggle between Sandbox (for testing) or Production (for real payments). Copy the Application ID and Access Token and paste them into the Square Gateway Settings tab above.

4

Get Your Location ID

Under Locations, copy your Location ID. Alternatively, simply click the "🔍 Test & Auto-Find Location" button in the settings tab above, and the system will automatically fetch all valid location IDs from your Square account!

5

Test with Square Test Cards (Sandbox)

In Sandbox mode, you can test with Square's official test credit card:
Card Number: 4111 1111 1111 1111 • Exp: any future date (e.g. 12/28) • CVV: 123 • Zip: 98101.
