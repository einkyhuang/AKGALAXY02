How to Connect Your Square Account

Follow these steps to connect your Square merchant account to your photography website.

1

Open Square Developer Portal

Go to developer.squareup.com and sign in with your regular Square merchant account.

2

Create or Select an Application

Click + New Application and enter a name (e.g. Taozijia Photography Web).

3

Copy Credentials

In the Credentials tab, copy the Application ID and Access Token into the Square Gateway section in the sidebar.

4

Auto-Find Location

Click the "🔍 Test & Auto-Find Location" button in the settings view to automatically verify and apply your merchant Location ID.
